── ABOUT

About DuckMath

We think every Malaysian student deserves good math practice — and that it should cost nothing.

命
── MISSION

Bad grades usually aren’t a talent problem.

For most Malaysian students the real obstacle isn’t talent — it’s no good practice and no quick feedback. Private tuition runs hundreds of ringgit a month. A textbook answer key tells you right or wrong, never why.

DuckMath fixes that. Pair a structured question bank with instant AI grading and every student — whatever their family earns — gets feedback tailored to their answer the second they hit submit. The duck and the level-up system are there to make it stick.

── OUR STORY

From prototype to platform

Start

A prototype, a database, a lot of coffee

DuckMath comes out of TinkTech — a small Malaysian edtech team. It began as a bare Streamlit prototype and a Supabase database, and a couple of weekends spent getting the first batch of questions to work.

Growth

Students kept asking for more

Within weeks, students were pushing teachers to add more questions. The duck level-up system came next — chasing a goal makes the practice go down easier.

Now

The full SC04–SC07 syllabus

DuckMath now covers the UEC math syllabus — SC04, SC05, SC06 and SC07 — with questions written and reviewed by experienced Malaysian math teachers, and a bank that keeps growing.
